# Surface brief · site (index.html + lockmachine.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitors: the TCC committee, professors, classmates, curious supervisors.
Job: understand in seconds what LockMachine does, believe it is a real working MVP (honestly labelled),
then open the simulator or the app. Constraints: no build step, self-hosted assets, CSP 'self',
PHP shared hosting, phones first-class, honesty rules from CLAUDE.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the shop floor at night. A polished epoxy floor under high-bay light, where the
product's verdict is a physical light (the machine's stack lamp and the reader LED) reflected in the
floor. Refuses the SaaS template (headline left + mockup right + row of equal cards) and the generic
"black with neon glowing edges": light only comes from sources that exist in the scene.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #06070A to #0C0E15 (dark anodized steel), panels as matte powder-coat with 1px
hairlines (rgba white .07 to .12), corners 18 to 28px, soft offset shadows, no hard block shadows.
Materials: anodized aluminium (cobalt and yellow halves of the Encaixe mark), black powder-coat,
polished epoxy. Light palette = Andon stack: green #22D07B, amber #FFD23F, red #FF3D2E, plus
cobalt #2F45FF as the LinkWave light. Text #ECEEF4 / #A3A9BD. Archivo 800 display, sentence case;
Martian Mono only for data (UIDs, codes, timestamps, readings).

STORY: The visitor sees the mechanism lit (card meets reader, the lamp answers green or red with
the reason), learns that every attempt is logged, and acts: test the simulator or open the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL scene behind the copy. Home: the two anodized halves of the
Encaixe mark lock together over the reflective floor, slow orbit following the pointer; headline
bottom-left at display size, primary action beside it, glass-free nav pill on top. LockMachine:
the RFID card meets the reader on a machine panel with a three-lamp stack tower; the floor
reflects the active lamp; a live verdict line under the headline is synced to the 3D cycle.

FORM: pinned by the user ("Escuro cinematográfico", 25/10/2026). Code-led (no image generation).
Signature interaction: the verdict cycle on LockMachine (the visitor can trigger it) and the
Encaixe lock on the home; the opening veil's hole reveals the lit scene.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
